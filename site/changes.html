Changes

What has changed on the Monster Lookup, newest first. Back to the monster list.

November 18, 2026

  • New site. Every creature in the 2014 SRD (5.1) and the 2024 SRD (5.2), with full stat blocks and a switch between the two editions. Creatures in both editions show a tab for each.
  • Search and filters. Search by name and filter by challenge rating range, type, size, alignment, damage resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities, condition immunities, legendary or lair, and fly, hover, swim, burrow or climb speeds. Each filter shows how many creatures it would leave. Sort by name, CR or hit points.
  • Stars and links. Star the creatures you use (saved in this browser) and show only those. The link keeps your filters, and an open stat block has its own link to share.
  • Creatures by CR chart. A bar chart shows how many of the matching creatures sit at each challenge rating. Hover or use the arrow keys to read a bar, or choose "Show as table".
  • More tools. The footer now links to the new Feats & Origins and Rules Reference sites.
