      About

      Every creature in the free System Reference Documents: the 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) and the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2), with full stat blocks. Pick the rules edition at the top; creatures that are in both editions show a tab for each in their stat block. Star the ones you use (saved in this browser), and copy the link to share a filtered list or a single stat block. The encounter builder adds up the XP of the creatures you pick and rates the fight for your party, by the 2024 XP budgets or the 2014 thresholds and multipliers.

      Stat blocks come from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. They were retrieved through the Open5e API, with 2024 sizes and lair XP checked against the 5e-bits database. Creatures from other books (the Monster Manuals and adventures) are not in the SRD and are left out. Unofficial fan content; not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.
